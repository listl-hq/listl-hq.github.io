What Listl does

Quick entry

Type it like you'd say it.

Write “2 kg apples, milk and 6 eggs” – Listl turns it into three items, with amount, unit and category, sorted by your way through the store.

  • Understands English and German – Austrian and Swiss words included
  • Suggestions from what you buy often; duplicates are added up
  • Photograph a handwritten list, paste text or bring lists over from Reminders

Together

One list for the whole household.

Share a household with family, flatmates or friends – or just a single list. Everyone sees changes instantly.

  • Invite with a link or QR code – people without an account join as guests
  • Roles for owners, admins, members and guests
  • “Anything else?” – let the others know you're going shopping, and they add things right from the notification

In the store

Your way through the store.

Shopping mode shows big rows in the order you walk the aisles – Listl learns it for every store and branch.

  • One item for several stores: bought once, done everywhere
  • A Live Activity on the Lock Screen shows what's next
  • If you like, a reminder when you arrive at one of your stores
  • Customer cards ready at the checkout – the store's card first
  • With Pro, Listl tells you when something on the list was bought only recently

Purchases and receipts

Receipts that read themselves.

Snap a receipt or share an e-receipt – Listl reads store, date, total and every price, right on your iPhone.

  • Bonus points, discounts and deposits at a glance
  • Reads the QR code on many receipts, takes receipts from a Mail automation and imports orders from Amazon and Migros
  • With Pro: what a list roughly costs, a budget, what you spend and how prices change

Recipes

From recipe to list.

Save recipes from websites or write your own. Change the servings, and Listl puts everything on your list with the right amounts.

  • Only what's missing: Listl counts what's already on the list
  • Each item shows the recipe it's for
  • Folders and favorites keep your recipes in order

Wherever you need it.

On your wrist, on the Home Screen, with Siri – often without even opening the app.

  • Apple Watch

    Check things off on your wrist and add by dictation.

  • Widgets

    A list, all lists or what you buy often – check off and add right there.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    “What's on my list in Listl?” – and check things off right in the Siri window.

  • Control Center

    Add something or go shopping with one press – also with the Action button.

  • Share from Safari

    Ingredients from a recipe straight onto a list.

  • Folders and favorites

    For lists and recipes – your favorites always on top.

Yours alone.

No ads, no sponsored products, no tracking. Your lists belong to you – even when you sync them, nobody else can read them.

  • No account needed

    Without an account, everything stays on your iPhone.

  • You choose where

    Only on this device, in your iCloud or on Listl's server in Frankfurt.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    In iCloud, in households and even in notifications. The server sees no names, items or prices.

  • Locked if you like

    App lock with Face ID and encrypted backups.
