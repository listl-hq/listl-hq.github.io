2. What Listl offers

(1) Listl is an app for shopping lists: add, check off and share – with stores, recipes, purchases and receipts. The app and its website describe what it currently does.

(2) You can use Listl without an account. Then your data lives only on your device or, if you choose, in your iCloud. To sync through our server and to share yourself, you sign in with the LaCodea ID, the account for all of LaCodea's apps. Anyone who is invited can also join as a guest without a LaCodea ID.

We may change Listl if there is a valid reason – such as new technology, security, a change in the law, changes to services we use, or new features –, if this causes you no additional costs and if we inform you clearly about the change. 3. Account, households and guests

(1) Keep access to your LaCodea ID and your Apple Account secret. If you notice any misuse, let us know right away.

(2) In a household or a shared list, all members see its lists, items, recipes, photos, purchases and receipts. Whoever invites chooses the role (admin, member or guest); owners and admins can remove members. Anyone who has an invitation link can join while it is valid – share it only with the people you want to invite.

(3) A guest account is tied to one iPhone. If that iPhone is lost before the guest has secured the account with a LaCodea ID, access to the guest account is gone. We delete guest accounts that haven't been used for twelve months.

(4) If you leave a household or delete your account, what you contributed there stays available to the other members.

4. Encryption and keys

(1) What Listl syncs through iCloud or our server, your device encrypts end to end. We can't read it – and therefore can't restore it either.

(2) Access to your data rests with your iCloud Keychain and your recovery key. If both are gone, nobody can decrypt your data, not even us. Keep your recovery key in a safe place and also back up your data, for example with a backup.

5. Listl Pro and Listl Max

(1) Listl is free. Listl Pro and Listl Max are subscriptions that unlock more – such as more lists, households, shared lists and people, more room for photos and receipts, statistics and design options. The app shows what each level includes before you buy.

(2) Both subscriptions are available monthly or yearly. You buy them through the App Store; Apple handles the payment, and Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions apply. You see the prices in the app before you buy, including VAT.

(3) Subscriptions renew automatically for the chosen period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, and for each renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period. After you cancel, the subscription stays active until the end of the paid period. You manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

(4) If you subscribe to Listl Pro for the first time, you can try it free for one week. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the free week, the paid subscription begins. Listl Max has no free week.

(5) You can share Listl Pro with up to five other family members through Apple's Family Sharing. Listl Max only applies to the Apple Account that subscribes.

(6) If you switch to a higher level, it applies immediately and Apple credits the rest of the current period; you switch to a lower level at the next renewal. Apple governs the details.

(7) A subscription is personal. How many people fit into a household or shared list and how much room there is for photos and receipts depends on the subscription of whoever created it. Joining is always free.

(8) When a subscription ends, your data is kept, and what is already shared stays shared. The limits of the free version apply again: if you're above them, you can't create anything new in that area until you're below them again or use a subscription again. Colors, app icon and the font for numbers go back to the default; with a new subscription, your settings come back.

(9) Because Apple handles the purchase, refunds and the right of withdrawal are governed by Apple's rules. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Your statutory rights remain unaffected.

This right ends when you delete the content or your account; what you contributed to a shared household stays there for the other members.

(2) Only add content you have the necessary rights to – for example photos and recipes from other sources –, and share photos in which other people can be recognized only with their consent.

(3) Store logos are trademarks of their owners. Listl only shows them so you recognize your stores; there is no affiliation with the retailers.

8. What Listl reads and suggests

Listl reads receipts, handwritten lists and recipes automatically and suggests items, amounts, routes and totals – on iPhones with Apple Intelligence also with its help, on the device. This can be wrong. Check recognized prices and totals before you rely on them; the list total and budget are estimates based on earlier prices. Listl is no replacement for bookkeeping.
