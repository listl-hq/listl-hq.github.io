Good to know

  • Prices in Germany, including VAT. In other countries, the App Store shows the price in your currency.
  • If you subscribe to Listl Pro for the first time, the first week is free. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, and you pay nothing.
  • With Apple's Family Sharing, up to five family members get your Listl Pro too. Listl Max applies to your Apple Account only.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. You manage and cancel them in your Apple Account settings.
  • Switching to a higher level applies right away, to a lower one at the next renewal.
  • How many people fit into a household and how much room it has depends on the subscription of whoever created it. Joining is always free.
  • If a subscription ends, nothing is lost: your data stays, and what's shared stays shared. More in the Terms of Use.
